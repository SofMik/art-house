THE ARTIST BEHIND THE CANVAS

Every Painting Begins with Delight

I have always painted, albeit with a few pauses along the way. Everything truly changed after several remarkable, almost fateful encounters with complete strangers. Their casual remarks and words pushed me forward in the most incomprehensible way, for which I am endlessly grateful. I did not know these people before those meetings, nor have I seen them since, yet they became the very impulse that led me to take lessons and reach a completely new chapter in my art.

Today, I live and create in Spain. The local southern character—generous sunshine, the play of light, and the architecture—has become an inexhaustible source of inspiration and directly influences my palette.

Through different styles and textures, I create original works that bring warmth, character and individuality into a space.

The spark for creating a piece is often genuine delight: a sudden glint of light or a deep shadow, the quiet beauty of everyday objects that create a sense of cosiness, or a candid photograph. My main subjects are frequently simple, familiar items in still lifes, or people—especially the elderly and children, who hold so much life, wisdom, and sincerity.

One day, I made a promise to myself to sit down at the easel only when in a good mood and feeling emotionally uplifted. I noticed a clear pattern: an artist’s thoughts and feelings inevitably charge the canvas with their energy and are reflected in the final result. Therefore, I sincerely hope that my paintings bring light, joy, and positive energy into this world.
